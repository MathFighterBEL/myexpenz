MyExpenz

A private expense tracker for iPhone. Keep track of your income, expenses, budgets, trips and net worth in one place, without ads or an account.

  • No account needed
  • No ads
  • Your data stays on your device
  • Available in 17 languages

Highlights

Everything at a glance

The dashboard shows your income, expenses, budgets, net worth and savings rate. Tap any number to see the charts and transactions behind it.

  • Income, expenses and savings rate per month or year
  • Net worth across all your accounts
  • Budget progress right on the dashboard

Budgets that follow your pay schedule

Budget per month, per category or per tag. Use calendar months, or a custom cycle that starts on the day you get paid.

  • Monthly and category budgets
  • Tag and trip budgets
  • Envelope budgets
  • Savings goals

Know what a trip really cost

Tag your transactions to a trip and MyExpenz breaks the cost down for you: per night, per meal, per activity or per fuel stop.

  • Compare trips side by side
  • Average cost per trip and per day
  • Use past trips to plan the next one

See where your money goes

Bar charts, time series, category breakdowns, a Sankey diagram that follows your income to where it's spent, and a heatmap of your spending days.

  • Filter by period, category, tag, payee, location or account
  • Automatic insights compared with last month
  • Table views with monthly and yearly averages

Add transactions your way

Income, expenses, transfers, debts and planned payments. Split a payment over several categories, add a payee, location, note or receipt, and set up recurring transactions for things like rent or subscriptions.

  • Quick entry with Siri and Shortcuts
  • Log Apple Pay payments with a shortcut
  • Import CSV files from your bank, with rules for payees and categories

All your accounts, in any currency

Bank, cash, savings, credit card, loan, deposit and real estate accounts, in more than 60 currencies. Transfers between accounts are kept separate, so moving money doesn't show up as spending.

  • Track what you owe and what others owe you
  • Include debts in your net worth, or keep them apart
  • Share selected accounts through iCloud and settle split costs

Your data stays yours

MyExpenz has no ads, no account and no servers of its own. Your financial data stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud backup or sharing. You can also lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode.
