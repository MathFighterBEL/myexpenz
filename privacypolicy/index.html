Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

MyExpenz is an expense tracker for iPhone. It works without an account, shows no ads and has no servers of its own. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

The short version

We do not collect any personal or financial data. Everything you enter in MyExpenz is stored on your device. It only leaves your device if you choose to turn on iCloud backup or share an account with someone through iCloud. In both cases the data is stored in iCloud, which is run by Apple, and we have no access to it. To convert between currencies, the app downloads exchange rates from two public services; no personal or financial data is sent with those requests.

Data we collect

None. MyExpenz has no server of its own and does not send your personal or financial data to us or to anyone else. The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. We do not collect any of the data types listed in Apple's App Privacy guidelines.

Where your data is stored

Your transactions, accounts, categories, tags, payees, locations, budgets, trips, debts, savings goals, notes and attached receipts are stored locally on your device. Your device's built-in data protection keeps this data encrypted when your iPhone is locked.

iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy of your data is stored in your private iCloud Drive. This copy is linked to your Apple ID and protected by Apple's iCloud security. We cannot read or access it. You can turn backup off at any time in the app settings, and remove existing backups from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

iCloud sharing

You can choose to share selected accounts with other people through iCloud. When you do, the data of those accounts (such as their transactions and split expenses) is stored in iCloud and is visible to the people you invite. Only the accounts you choose to share are included. You can stop sharing at any time, after which the other people lose access to that data. Sharing is handled by Apple's iCloud service; we do not receive or store any shared data.

Location

MyExpenz can attach your current location to a transaction. This is optional and only works if you give permission through the iOS location prompt. Location data is stored with the transaction on your device and, if you use iCloud backup or sharing, in iCloud as described above. It is never sent to us. You can turn location access on or off at any time in your device's Settings. The app works fully without it.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Pay logging

You can add transactions with Siri and App Shortcuts, including shortcuts that log Apple Pay payments. Voice requests are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. MyExpenz only receives the transaction details the shortcut passes to it, such as the amount and merchant name. The app never has access to your card numbers or payment credentials.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on App Lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode and only tells MyExpenz whether the check succeeded. The app never has access to your biometric data.

Widgets

Widgets show data that is already stored on your device, such as your balance and recent spending. Keep in mind that Lock Screen widgets can be seen by anyone who has your phone, even when it is locked. You can remove them at any time.

Import and export

CSV files you import are processed on your device. Files you export are saved or shared wherever you choose, for example in the Files app or by email. Once exported, those files fall outside the app's control.

Exchange rates

MyExpenz supports more than 60 currencies. To convert amounts, the app downloads exchange rates from these services:

  • Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), a free, open-source API with rates from central banks, including the European Central Bank.
  • Open Exchange Rates (openexchangerates.org), operated by Open Exchange Rates (UK) Limited. See their privacy policy.

These requests only ask for rates for certain currencies and dates. They do not contain your transactions, balances, accounts or any other data you enter in the app. Like any request over the internet, they do reveal your device's IP address to the service. Frankfurter states that it does not log IP addresses. Open Exchange Rates may keep technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs, as described in its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

Apart from the exchange rate services above, MyExpenz only uses services that are built into iOS: iCloud (for backup and sharing) and Siri and Shortcuts. These are provided by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. No analytics tools or advertising frameworks are used.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can delete individual items or all data from within the app, or delete the app to remove all local data. Data in iCloud is removed by turning off iCloud backup or sharing and deleting the data from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or process your personal data, we don't hold any data about you that we could show, correct or delete. You have direct control over everything: you can view, change, export and delete your data in the app at any time.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights such as access, correction, deletion and data portability, and the right to file a complaint with your data protection authority. If you have any question about how these rights apply, contact us using the address below.

Children

MyExpenz is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect data from children, and in practice we do not collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also mention it in the app.
